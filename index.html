Greco

Greco is a personal desktop shell written by one person for use on their own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Privacy policy

Greco connects to the author's own Google account only to read the author's own calendar, so upcoming events can be shown on the author's own screens. It does not send, sell, share or publish any data to anyone. Calendar data is copied to and kept only on the author's own computer, and it can be deleted at any time by deleting that folder or revoking access in the Google account's security settings.

Greco does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers or any information about anyone else.
